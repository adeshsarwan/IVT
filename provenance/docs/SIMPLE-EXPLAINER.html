Traffic Provenance, explained simply

Did the affiliate's visitors really come from the Google / Meta ads they say they bought? A one-page tour of what we built, with a picture of how the gclid check works.

In one minute

  • Problem: an affiliate can send bots or porn traffic and claim it's Google traffic. Google then punishes our GAM account.
  • Idea: every Google ad click gets a unique ID, the gclid. Google keeps a list of every gclid it billed. Real visitors carry a gclid that's on that list. Fake ones don't.
  • Check: every 5,000 visits per affiliate, compare our gclids with Google's list (Meta: compare counts) and run a few more checks.
  • Result: OK / WATCH / THROTTLE / BLOCK. BLOCK is a recommendation: a person reviews it and turns on the kill switch ads:aff:<id> = off by hand (no automatic blocking for now).
  • Status: 🕒 logic built + tested locally on branch chandra/traffic-provenance. Not pushed, not live. The Google pipeline (ClickHouse + batch job) runs locally against a fake Google API; no real Google token yet.

1 · The life of a gclid

How one click becomes something we can verify.

A bot can type ?gclid=anything into a URL. It can't make Google put that ID in the affiliate's click list. That's the whole trick.

2 · How the matching works

Our visit log on the left, Google's click list on the right. Every gclid ends in exactly one bucket.

Google's list always has a few clicks that never reached us (people closing the tab). That's normal, and a useful tamper check: a partner-sent "click list" with zero such clicks was probably built from our own logs.

3 · The other checks

📊 Visits ÷ clicks

Per day: our "Google" visits vs Google's click count. Above 1.05× means extra traffic is being mixed in. Above 1.5× → BLOCK.

🔁 Same gclid, many IPs

One real click replayed by bots ("click laundering"). Over 2 IPs or 5 hits per ID = reused.

🔞 Bad placements

Real Google clicks bought on porn sites, junk apps, Search Partners or Display. Caught from Google's network/placement values.

⏱️ Timing & device

Landing before the click, landing days later, or Google says "desktop" while the browser is a phone.

🌙 24/7 traffic

People sleep; bot farms don't. A flat hour-of-day curve is flagged.

🧾 Report tampering

For partner-sent reports: duplicate or malformed IDs, future dates, numbers that copy our counts, or Google values that disagree with our URL.

Meta (Facebook / Instagram) is different

Also checked: Audience Network share (policy says FB/IG feeds and stories only), unknown ad IDs, and fbclid present/valid/not reused. fbclid can't be looked up, so it's evidence only.

  • Recommended default: Google → Ads Script push; Meta → partner token. Move to MCC + developer token when approved.
  • Self-reported files can prove traffic is bad, never fully prove it's good. They keep a WATCH floor and get spot audits more often (weak 25% / script 10% / API 2% of partners a week).
  • No access at all → at least THROTTLE (reduced ads).
  • Scales to 100+ partners: per-partner keys, staggered sync schedule with retries, one job per affiliate (one failure never blocks the others), and a status table per partner.

5 · From numbers to the kill switch

  • No guessing: under 300 visits, or Google data not in yet → no decision, tier stays as it is.
  • Unblock is manual. After 3 clean windows the system says "ready to unblock"; a person decides.
  • The code never writes to KV itself. BLOCK lands in a review queue; a person sets the key by hand.

6 · The daily batch job (Google)

Every click Google recorded, every gclid we logged, matched in one database. Runs every 6 hours, one partner at a time.
